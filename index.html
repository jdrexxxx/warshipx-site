WarshipX · a survival MMO in the Age of Sail

Everything in WarshipX

A survival MMO in the Age of Sail (about 1500–1720), on one ocean that circles a mini version of the real world. Every ship is wood, and every region grows its own woods. One sealed island, the Isle, is run as an extraction raid. Crews run their ships by hand, in first person: the helm, the sails, the guns, the carpenter's repairs, the pumps. This page shows everything the game will have, and the road from the first beach to the whole world.

What you do

The woods of the world

Every region grows its own woods, and no region grows them all. The woods you build with decide a ship's speed, strength, shot resistance, repair cost and lifespan, and every piece of timber has a grade (common, fine, prime) and a seasoning (green, seasoned, well seasoned). These are the woods of the first regions; every new region adds its own.

Six sloops: same plan, different woods

100 = the northern sloop of oak and pine. Life is in seasons (8 weeks each) until the hull is down to a quarter of its soundness. Best in each column in green, worst in orange. Example values: the final balance may differ.

The Timber Run. The best hull woods grow in the tropics and the best masts, tar and rope in the north, so a Havana hull with Norwegian masts is faster and safer aloft than the all-Caribbean sloop, and far tougher than the all-northern one. That trade, hardwoods north and masts south, is the backbone of the world's economy.

Survival, kept quick

Fight for land

Land can be taken by force: whole territories by companies, single plots by anyone. The fighting happens in the defender's war window, so nobody loses their land while asleep.

      Smuggling, piracy and the Crown

      Smugglers run untaxed rum and tobacco and forbidden goods for the biggest profits in the game, and pirates take ships at sea. A player-run Crown and its navy hunt them both and get paid for every seizure. Everyone else lives between the two: stay honest, carry for the smugglers, or sell information.

          The road map

          The game is built in stages, one at a time, and each stage has to be fun before the next one starts.

          Crafting: from a dugout to a man-of-war

          Five places to make things, from your own two hands to a shipyard. Materials are hauled in by boat, cart or mule train, crafting takes real time, timber and mill power, and the best recipes need plans from the Isle.

          The two biggest trees: weapons and ships

          Every gun and every ship is built from parts, so each one is a chain of trades: timber, iron, copper and hemp from the market, parts from workshops, plans from the world and the Isle.
