Everything in WarshipX

A survival MMO on one ocean that circles a mini version of the real world, with one sealed island, the Isle, run as an extraction shooter. This is everything the game will have, and the order we are building it in.

      Crafting: from a raft to a warship

      It follows the game's rules: materials are hauled in by boat, crafting takes real time, power and fuel, and the best recipes need parts from the Isle.

      The two biggest trees: weapons and boats

      Every gun and every boat is built from parts, so each one is a chain of trades: materials from the market, parts from workshops, blueprints from the world and the Isle.
